1. Loan Eligibility

Applicants seeking a personal loan must meet the lender's eligibility requirements. Eligibility may depend on age, income, employment status, credit history, and repayment capacity.

2. Required Documents

Applicants may be asked to provide identity verification, address verification, income proof, and employment-related documents. The exact requirements depend on the lender's official policy.

3. Credit Assessment

The lender evaluates the applicant's credit history, existing financial obligations, income stability, and ability to repay the loan.

4. Loan Approval

Loan approval is subject to document verification, credit assessment, and the lender's applicable policies.

5. Important Disclaimer

This document is fictional and intended only for software development and demonstration. It is not an official banking policy or regulatory guideline.
